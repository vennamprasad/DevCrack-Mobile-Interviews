## 🧠 Interview Process
1. **Round 1**: Technical Screening (Core Kotlin/Swift/Flutter concepts, OOP, Design Patterns).
2. **Round 2**: Hands-On Coding & Architecture Round (MVVM, Coroutines/async-await, Room/CoreData, Unit Testing).
3. **Round 3**: Client Scenario & System Design (Designing a large-scale airline mobile app or e-commerce retail app).
4. **Round 4**: Techno-Managerial Round (Stakeholder management, sprint planning, delivery ownership).

---

## 📝 Common Technical Questions
- [ ] **SOLID Principles in Mobile:** Concrete mobile examples of Single Responsibility, Open-Closed, and Dependency Inversion in Android/iOS apps.
- [ ] **Modernizing Legacy Codebases:** Step-by-step strategy for migrating an imperative XML/UIKit codebase to Jetpack Compose/SwiftUI without full rewrites.
- [ ] **Dependency Injection (Hilt / Dagger / Koin):** How scoping (`@Singleton`, `@ViewModelScoped`) works, and resolving circular dependency errors.
- [ ] **Multi-Threaded Synchronization:** Preventing race conditions in local caches when multiple background tasks update data simultaneously.
